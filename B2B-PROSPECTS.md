# Grounded Drops — B2B Prospect Hit-List & Outreach Kit (South Australia)

The working document for the "free coffee, no strings" campaign and the wholesale/office pipeline.
Named businesses researched July 2026 — verify contact details before sending anything.
Companion tracker: `b2b-prospects-tracker.csv` (pre-loaded with every prospect below).

**The one qualifying question that sorts every prospect:** *"Do you have a coffee machine on
site?"* → Yes = beans pipeline (ForestrySA lookalike). No = drip-bag crib-box pipeline. Ask it on
the phone before a box ships; it takes 15 seconds and doubles conversion because the right product
lands.

## The outreach kit

### Free-box card (print on your thermal/label setup)

> **G'day — this one's on us.**
> Ads are getting stupidly expensive, so we're spending the money on free coffee for hard-working
> crews instead. No strings, no follow-up spam — just good coffee roasted here in SA.
> We keep ForestrySA's crews running on it. If the crew rates it and you'd like crew/office
> pricing, scan the QR or call Richard: [phone].
> — Grounded Drops, roasted & packed in Australia
>
> `[QR → /crew page]` · Box code: `[UNIQUE-CODE]`

(The unique code per box tells you which boxes convert. Get named-them permission from ForestrySA
before printing.)

### Phone script (qualify BEFORE shipping a box)

> "G'day, it's Richard from Grounded Drops — we're a small SA coffee roaster, we supply
> ForestrySA's crews. Quick one: does your [office/depot/showroom] have a coffee machine on site?
> ... [Yes → beans sample] [No → drip-bag crib box] ...
> We're sending free trial boxes to a handful of SA [industry] businesses this month instead of
> paying for ads — no strings. Who should I address it to so it actually reaches the smoko room?"

That last question gets you the buyer's name — which is the real point of the call.

### Follow-up call (day 7 after delivery)

> "It's Richard from Grounded Drops — the coffee box. Did it land? What did the crew reckon?"
> [good] → "Want me to put together crew pricing? Most depots do a standing monthly box so nobody
> has to think about it." [meh] → "Fair enough — what do you normally drink? [note it, move on]"

### One-to-one follow-up email (Spam Act: conspicuous-publication basis)

Only to a published business address, personally written, role-relevant, one at a time, with your
details and a "tell me to stop and I will" line. No lists, no automation, no blasts. Record where
you found the address.

> Subject: the coffee box we dropped off at [Company]
> Hi [Name], Richard from Grounded Drops here — we sent the free coffee box to your
> [depot/office] last week. No strings as promised, but if the crew liked it, here's the
> crew-pricing one-pager: [link/attachment]. We already keep ForestrySA's crews caffeinated and
> we're an SA roaster, so support/refills are same-week. If coffee's not your department or you'd
> rather not hear from me, just say so and that's the end of it. Cheers, Richard [phone]

### The sell sheet (one page, take everywhere)

Logo · product photos · the ForestrySA line · crew/office price list (beans per kg tiers + drip
crib-box 50-pack) · "roasted in SA" · reorder QR · your mobile. Nothing else.

## Cadence (fits around packing orders)

- **5 qualify calls/day** (Tier 3/4 list) → ship boxes only to qualified, named recipients
- **5 boxes/week** ($75–125/wk all-in) → **day-7 follow-up call on every box, no exceptions**
- **1 Tier 1 machine-vendor conversation/week** (in person, take samples)
- **1 Tier 5/6 pitch/fortnight** (coworking kitchen or council depot)
- Track everything in `b2b-prospects-tracker.csv` — a box with no follow-up call is a donation,
  not marketing.

At a 1-in-10 box-to-account rate (conservative with qualification + follow-up), 5 boxes/week ≈
2 new recurring accounts/month. Twenty ForestrySA-sized accounts is a business-changing revenue
floor that no algorithm can take away from you.
