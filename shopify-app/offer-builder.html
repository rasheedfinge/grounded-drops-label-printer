      Where do I find a product “handle” or variant ID?

      Product handle: open the product on your storefront — the last part of the URL. For groundeddrops.com/products/ground-coffee the handle is ground-coffee.

      Variant ID (only needed when a gift product has multiple variants and you want a specific one): open yourstore.com/products/<handle>.js in a browser tab — each entry under variants has an id. Alternatively in Shopify admin, open the product, click the variant — the number at the end of the URL is the variant ID.

      Remember: every offer here also needs its matching native discount so the gift is actually free — see SETUP.md, Part B.
