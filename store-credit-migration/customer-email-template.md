# Customer heads-up email (send before cancelling Rise)

For your <10 wallet customers. The only thing changing for them is *how*
they redeem — the balance carries over exactly, and there's no longer a code
to remember.

---

**Subject:** A quick update about your Grounded Drops wallet

Hi {{first_name}},

We've upgraded how your prepaid wallet works — and it's now even easier to use.

Your remaining balance of **{{balance}}** has been moved directly onto your
Grounded Drops account. From now on, there's **no code to enter**:

1. Add your items to the cart and head to checkout.
2. **Sign in** to your account (or check out with Shop Pay).
3. Your wallet balance appears automatically as **"Store credit"** — just
   tap to apply it.

That's it. Your balance draws down on each order until it's used up, and you
can always see what's left when you're logged into your account.

If you have any trouble at checkout, just reply to this email and we'll sort
it out right away.

Thanks for being one of our wallet members 🤎
The Grounded Drops Team
